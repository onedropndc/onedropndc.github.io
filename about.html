About One Drop

One Drop is a volunteer initiative by Notre Dame College, Dhaka, Batch 27 students. It helps people find donors and share blood requests.

Why we built it

Connecting people who need blood with voluntary donors

We are not a blood bank. We do not collect, screen, store or transport blood. Contact details in the directory are publicly accessible; please read the privacy policy before registering.

Our principles: Donation should be voluntary and unpaid. Moderators may review or remove misleading, commercial or inappropriate listings.

What One Drop does

  • ✓A searchable donor directory with blood-group, district and distance filters
  • ✓A live emergency board with countdown timers
  • ✓Browser alerts may appear while the site is open
  • ✓Contact numbers are accessible in the public directory data
  • ✓A display filter based on the last donation date
  • ✓An installable app with limited offline access to cached information

What it does not do

  • Store, screen or supply blood
  • Replace a hospital blood bank or a doctor’s decision
  • Charge a fee for using the site
