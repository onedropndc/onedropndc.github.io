Before you register

  • Confirm age, weight and other eligibility requirements with the blood bank.
  • Ask the blood bank how long to wait between donations.
  • Tell the blood bank about fever, illness, medicines and recent donations.
  • Eat normally and drink plenty of water beforehand.
  • Bring your national ID or birth certificate to the blood bank.

Remember: The blood bank or clinician makes the final decision about your eligibility.
