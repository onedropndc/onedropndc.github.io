Privacy policy, terms and contact

Privacy policyTerms of useContact the team

Please review how your information is used. Last updated: 2 November 2026.

1. What we store

Donor profiles include your name, phone, blood group, district, availability and any optional details you provide, including GPS if you choose to share it. New emergency requests include blood group, units, hospital, district, contact number and needed-by time. Older records may contain additional details.

Contact forms may collect the email address or phone number you provide. Staff authentication uses email and password. Please avoid sharing medical records or identity numbers in free-text fields.

2. What is public

Visible to any visitor: your name, blood group, district/area, availability status, donation count, age and note. Your mobile number, optional GPS coordinates and other donor record fields are accessible in the public directory data. The interface masks the number until a visitor taps “Show contact”, but this is not a security control. The per-browser limit can be bypassed.

Emergency request data, including the contact number, may also be publicly accessible while a request is awaiting review. Do not submit someone else’s information without permission.

Only submit information you are comfortable sharing publicly. Avoid medical records, identity numbers and other sensitive details in free-text fields.

3. Your location

Precise location is optional. The interface may show approximate distance, but shared GPS coordinates are accessible in directory data. Without GPS, the district centre is used as a rough estimate.

4. How to change or delete your data

My donor profile: open it in the browser used to register. When connected, you can edit, mark yourself unavailable or remove the listing. Marking yourself unavailable does not make your record private. If you lose access, contact the team; recovery is not guaranteed.

5. Security

Data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore. Directory data is public; updates and deletion depend on authentication and the security rules actually deployed. The proposed rules are in the repository and must be reviewed and tested before publishing.

6. Moderation

Volunteer moderators may review and remove misleading, duplicate, commercial or abusive listings and requests. Review does not guarantee that every detail has been independently verified.

7. Children

One Drop is intended for donors aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger.

8. Terms of use

  • Provide accurate information. The blood bank or clinician determines medical eligibility.
  • Donation must always be voluntary and unpaid. Never ask for or accept payment.
  • Do not post false, duplicate or commercial emergency requests.
  • Do not use the directory for marketing, spam or harassment.
  • One Drop helps people connect; it is not a medical service. The clinician and blood bank make transfusion decisions.
  • In a life-threatening emergency, call the hospital blood bank and emergency services first.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page and its update date will be revised.

10. Contact the team

Found incorrect information or have a suggestion? Send the team a message. Please do not include sensitive medical or identity details.
