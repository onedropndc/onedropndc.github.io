Eligibility

Who can donate blood?
The blood bank checks age, weight, haemoglobin and other requirements under applicable guidance. Ask the blood bank if you are unsure.
When should I speak to the blood bank before donating?
Tell the blood bank about illness, infection, medicines, recent surgery, tattoos, pregnancy, breastfeeding or recent donations. The medical team will advise you.
How often can I donate?
One Drop currently uses a 56-day display filter. This is not a medical eligibility decision and does not automatically change a donor’s availability. Confirm the appropriate interval with the blood bank or clinician.

Before and during donation

What should I do before donating?
Eat a normal meal, drink enough water and rest beforehand. Follow the blood bank’s instructions and bring any identification they request.
Does it hurt? Will I feel weak?
You may feel a brief needle prick or light-headedness. Tell the medical staff immediately if you feel unwell; they can help you.
What should I know about the equipment?
Ask the staff to confirm that sterile, single-use equipment is used. Discuss any safety concerns with them before donating.

Using One Drop

Do I need an account to search for donors?
No. You can browse donors, requests and compatibility information without registering. To be listed as a donor, submit a profile; no email or password is required.
Is my phone number visible to everyone?
Your number is masked on the card, but it is accessible in the public directory data. The per-browser reveal limit can be bypassed and is not a security control.
How do I stop appearing in search results?
My donor profile: open it in the browser used to register. Choose “Remove my listing” to delete it. “Mark as busy” changes your availability but does not make your record private. Changes need a connection.
Why does “Nearest to me” sometimes look wrong?
When a donor allowed location access, distance uses their shared coordinates. When they did not, we fall back to the centre of their district — an estimate, and labelled as such. The card always shows the district so you can judge for yourself.
Does One Drop work without internet?
Some previously cached pages and information may be available offline, but they may be outdated. New requests and profile changes need a connection.
Is there a WhatsApp group or community?
Yes. You can join the One Drop WhatsApp community to connect with volunteers and share requests. Joining is optional, and you can leave at any time. Confirm urgent details with the hospital.

Is this free? Who pays for it?
Using One Drop is free. It is a student-led volunteer initiative.

Medical disclaimer: One Drop is an information and coordination tool. It does not provide medical advice, does not screen donors and does not replace a hospital blood bank. Always follow the instructions of the treating doctor and the blood bank staff.

Quick checklist for donors

  • Eat a normal meal beforehand
  • Drink enough water
  • Get enough rest
  • Tell staff about illness and medicines
  • Ask the blood bank about donation intervals
  • Bring any identification requested
